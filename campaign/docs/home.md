---
title: Naadag Hasaka
subtitle: Cahokia, 710 · a saga for Coyote & Crow
---

Grandmother Naadag came to Cahokia from the Diné Republic as a young woman, and nearly a hundred and
twenty-one years after she was born she chose the day she would die. Her family held a Seeing for her:
ten days of arrivals, long tables, stories and farewells, with her death at dawn on the seventh.

Four people came to it by different roads. Suva, who had married into the family forty years before,
was to attend her at the end. Datsu brought the food. Sign followed a vision across the Free Lands
without knowing whose it was. Makatooka came back to a family she had drifted away from. Tika came as his
sister's guest and left with a job.

Before she died, Grandmother asked them to carry a box back to the Diné Republic.

*Naadag Hasaka* is Chahi for *Crow Mesa*.
