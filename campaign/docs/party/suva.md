A healer whose work is death and trauma: the ceremonies at the end of a life and the spiritual side of
medicine. Her own family died in a fire when she was in her early twenties. She married into
Grandmother Naadag's family, farmers, and has been part of it for forty years. She and her husband
are raising their grandchildren, Wasawi and her younger brother.

Grandmother asked her to attend her at the end of her Seeing. It was the first one Suva had led. At the
long tables Grandmother gave her the family's heirloom jewellery and called her the closest thing to a
daughter she had left.

She is taking Wasawi with her on the journey west.
