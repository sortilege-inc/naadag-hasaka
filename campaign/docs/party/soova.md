A healer whose work is death and trauma: the ceremonies at the end of a life and the spiritual side of
medicine. She came to Cahokia as a young woman. Her family, she says, died in a fire, and she carries a
scar on her face. She married Hoyohih, a farmer and one of Grandmother Naadag's sons, and has been part
of the family for close to forty years. Their daughter, Kii Das, died two years ago. Soova and Hoyohih
are raising her children, Wazawi and Loohok.

She speaks Chahi and Diné. She can walk the Black.

Grandmother asked her to attend her at the end of her Seeing. It was the first one Soova had led. At the
long tables Grandmother gave her the family's heirloom jewellery and called her the closest thing to a
daughter she had left.

She is taking Wazawi with her on the journey west.
