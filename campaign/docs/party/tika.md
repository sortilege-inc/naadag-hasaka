Unlad, from the Haudenosaunee Confederacy. He left home with his older sister Yohipa when they had no
family left there, and in Cahokia she runs a restaurant he used to work in. He is gangly and frail, with
cybernetic eyes and a small drone that follows him around. He trained in martial arts under a master and
knows the technique better than his body can do it. He loves cooking, and loves games more; he wins
large sums and pushes his luck until he loses them again.

He wants to build cybernetics that give a person what the Adanadi gives. Yohipa asked Grandmother Naadag
to find him a place on the journey west, and he was hired on as an escort.
