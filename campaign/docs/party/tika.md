A tinkerer from the Haudenosaunee Confederacy who came to Cahokia with his older sister Yohipa. He has a
cybernetic eye he keeps pushing past its limits and a small drone that follows him around. He has some
judo, loves cooking, and loves games more, and he has lost a lot of money gambling.

He wants to build cybernetics that give a person what the Adanadi gives. He won his place on the journey
west, as apprentice mechanic, in a dice game at the Seeing.
