A Seeker from the Diné Republic who has spent part of her life around criminal organisations and now
cares most about justice. She goes deep into the Black when she investigates, sometimes deep enough
that she stops hearing what is said to her.

She was looking into a wrong done to a poor man in Coyote City when someone reached her through the
Black with the name of a family. She dropped the case and hitchhiked to Cahokia, and arrived in time for
Grandmother Naadag's Seeing. Grandmother told her she had a gift for her that would have to wait; at the
long tables she gave her a necklace.

On the tenth day she would not wake.
