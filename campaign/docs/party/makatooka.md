A physician on leave from her hospital, trained in medicine and the sciences more than ceremony. She is
altruistic, and she is fighting an addiction. She has a hairless cat called Wings.

Years ago she was close to Grandmother Naadag's family through Zibizin, who taught her to fly, and she
drifted away after Zibizin went missing. She came to the Seeing to start mending that. Grandmother's gift
to her was a blanket.
