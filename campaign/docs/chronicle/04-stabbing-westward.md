## What they took

The credit Grandmother's family had arranged bought a good deal. A medical kit, with a paralytic,
surgical knives and stabilisers, and first aid besides. A couple of small gats. Tents, sleeping bags,
blankets and backpacks. A charging station and a portable one. Six communicators. A heavy mag sling and
a handheld one. An advanced toolkit for cybernetics. Things for cooking.

Their way lay west, toward Coyote City and past it, by a song that had not been sung in a generation.

## The quail and the fox

Syn had gone under during the quiet days of the Seeing, the fifth, sixth and seventh, which are set
apart for reflection, meditation and consolation. Trances were somewhat familiar to them. This one was
not. They were in the Black, but nowhere they knew it, and they had the form of a quail. They were
following a fox into a forest.

They kept a safe distance as the fox went deeper in. The light behind them filtered through the leaves,
and the forest closed in, and grew darker and darker. The fox stopped at a clearing, facing an old
stone standing upright, long and narrow. Syn waited at the edge of the clearing.

On the road, Soova tended Syn's body. She reached for them through the spirit, and found them. She saw
a clearing of standing stones, cut with inscriptions she could not read. The fox darted away. The quail
flew up and settled on top of the stone, and the glyphs shifted and rearranged themselves; Soova still
could not read them. She touched the rock. There were three beings there: the quail, Soova, and the
stone, which had a being of its own.

Then they both woke. Soova explained where they were, and that they had brought Syn with them. Syn was
worried about the box. Soova did not believe Grandmother would give them something that meant them
harm.

## Close quarters

A yutsu bus is a small place to share. Migatuka wanted to know what had really happened to Zibizin. She
also had an idea: Tika had been showing off his drone, and she thought it could scout the riverbanks
for places where kelera root might be growing wild.

Wazawi tried to defend what she had done, and that annoyed Soova. Tika had been showing Wazawi his
cybernetics, and Wazawi looked up to him; he seemed to her like someone who knew things. Between Soova
and Tika there was bad blood.

Daatsu did not think the box was ominous or dangerous. He did have the sense that if it were opened
there would be no going back. It might be a puzzle box.

## Hiicho

They were making camp when Migatuka found a boy among the boxes, about seven years old, quietly playing
something like jacks. He did not speak. He taught her the game patiently, without a word. It was clear
that he had been helping himself to the supplies.

He signed, and Daatsu, who knows Plains sign, read his name: Hiicho.

There was a great deal of argument about what to do. The boy explained that he was excited to go on
the big adventure to the mountains. His friend's family had travelled, and his own family could not,
so he had decided to come with them. His family was back in Cahokia.

There were two other parties near them on the way: a few shippers, and a larger party heading away
from Cahokia. The signal was weak, but they got a message back to Cahokia about the boy. Eventually an
answer came: stay where you are, an escort is being sent.

The saga went on for two more sessions, on 16 and 30 June 2023; the second was interrupted. Neither
was recorded.
