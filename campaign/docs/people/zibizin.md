Kii Das's partner and Wazawi's parent, and a friend of Migatuka's who taught her to fly; they met when
Wazawi shattered her shin exploring. Zibizin set off to explore seven years ago and did not return. Their
blanket, the falcon reworked into a fox, was Grandmother's gift to Migatuka.
