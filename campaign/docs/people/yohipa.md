Tika's older sister, about twenty-five. She brought him from the Haudenosaunee Confederacy to Cahokia and
runs a successful restaurant there, which Grandmother Naadag helped her start. Her kitchen helped feed the
Seeing. She owes Tika money for his work and will pay him when he is back.
