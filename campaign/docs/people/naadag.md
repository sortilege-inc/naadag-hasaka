name: Grandmother Naadag
order: 1
role: The elder whose Seeing it was
chapters: [1, 2, 3]
---

Nearly a hundred and twenty-one. She came to Cahokia from the Diné Republic in her late adolescence,
married there, and built a family and a neighbourhood around her; a member of the Council of Twelve came
to her Seeing. She was more ceremonial than spiritual. The story of her life told on the fourth day left
her early years thin, with gaps left there on purpose. She died at dawn on the seventh day and was buried
beside her husband.
