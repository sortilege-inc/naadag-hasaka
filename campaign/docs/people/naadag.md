Nearly a hundred and twenty-one. She was born in the Diné Republic; during the All Tribes War the man who
became her husband, Misyooyi, found her on his way back, and she came to Cahokia with him in her
adolescence. She had not been to Diné since she was a child. Misyooyi died about fifteen years before
her. Their children include Hoyohih, Soova's husband.
She built a family and a neighbourhood around her; a member of the Council of Twelve came to her Seeing.
She was more ceremonial than spiritual. The story of her life told on the fourth day left her early years
thin, with gaps left there on purpose. She died at dawn on the seventh day and was buried beside her
husband. Her last request was that a sealed box be carried back to her ancestral lands, in Oraibi.
