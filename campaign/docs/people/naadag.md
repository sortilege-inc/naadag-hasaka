name: Grandmother Naadag
order: 1
role: The elder whose Seeing it was
chapters: [1, 2, 3]
---

Nearly a hundred and twenty-one. She came to Cahokia from the Diné Republic in her late adolescence and
married Misyooyi, who died twenty-one years before her. Their children include Hoyohih, Soova's husband.
She built a family and a neighbourhood around her; a member of the Council of Twelve came to her Seeing.
She was more ceremonial than spiritual. The story of her life told on the fourth day left her early years
thin, with gaps left there on purpose. She died at dawn on the seventh day and was buried beside her
husband. Her last request was that a box be carried to Oraibi.
