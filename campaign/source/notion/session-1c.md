Tika: 19, gangly, frail. Cybernetic eye. Small drone that follows.
Sister running a restuarant.
Gambling addict. Obsessed with games. Lost a lot of money (30k ish?)
“Martial artist”; have the knowledge but not great strength.
Cooking, cybernetics with adanadi.
Daatsu may have supplied Tika’s sister’s restaurant.
She was catering a portion of the Seeing. Tika came in to assist for a bit.
Tika gets embroiled in a conversation with someone who is hears their theories about reproducing the adanadi’s effects synthetically; they (Choyan) are skeptical. They talk through why they think that won’t work, and expose Tika to the more academic sources.
Tika gets shut out of some of the naasi games with the uncles. But the sports betting with favors is pretty typical.
Tika’s bet wins well, he ends up winning a job servicing a yutsu heading west. Paid 1/3 up front. Also will cook.
Migatuka is really feeling the loss of Zibizin, the loss of her miscarriage, the incoming loss of Naadag. She begins to have a vision. She sees her ‘spiritual’ experiences as manifesting from her subconscious. She feels herself looking through someone’s eyes, her eyes. She’s humming a tune. There’s a pot for kelera tea. She pours the hot tea into a drinking bowl. It’s far too large. There’s a voice from the other room, which sounds familiar, she can’t make it out. She looks back, and there’s an infant, presumably her child, under the water as the tea keeps pouring in. It starts to come up for air. She throws away the pot, turns back and the bowl is empty. She wakes up from the trip, sweating, it was really intense.
Migatuka was in a room with others sleeping, reading quietly, meditating, and she wakes with a start. Tries to keep her distress to herself, unsuccessfully. The others clearly notice but pretend like they don’t.
Soova and her assistant ready Grandmother Naadag. Massages, sage, trimming nails, braiding hair. Song plays a prominent role. There are namings of spirits, of ancestors, as guides.
Soova was anxious about all of this, but all of that prep pays off, it feels pretty natural. She is honored by the level of trust Grandmother Naadag is placing in her. It’s an emotional marathon, being so close to someone facing death in such a real way.
Towards the end of day six, Soova notices the door is ajar. The sleep is punctuated, vigil is held. Soova closes it, makes a note to ask about it.
The method of death is burning a combination of plants that produce an incredibly toxic and fatal smoke, but it’s trivially negated with appropriate medicine. It will slow and then stop Grandmother’s physical processes.
Tika tries showing off a kind of martial art in the park, sparring with others, but clearly can’t hold his own.
Soova is wearing the jewelry for the day of death. Senses something off. Uses Walk the Black to get a sense if there’s a bad spirit or something that might compromise the ritual. Has a sense of auras, but can interact slightly. Presses her sense forward, can see an outline of an aura, almost infrared, in the closet. Her astral body moves into the closet, and she finds Wazawi, body sore and tensed, trying to stretch some muscles individually. Trying to stay awake, struggling, can’t sleep.
Soova is mad. Big mad. She also knows that if she hadn’t found her it would’ve been fatal.
As she returns to her body, she plays things out in her head; she can’t compromise the ceremony. It would dishonor the family to reveal the situation, but she’s willing to accept that, but the ceremony is more important.
Soova goes and prepares the antidote, and opens the closet to a stupored Wazawi. In an urgent whisper, she gives Wazawi the medicine and scolds her for how unacceptable this was, and that she will stay in the closet until everything is finished. She gives Wazawi some water and closes the closet again.
Wazawi is too exhausted to have any will to fight, and meekly complies.
At dawn, Soova wakes her assistant and Grandmother, and they greet the dawn one last time, before settling in holding hands as the tendrils of smoke begin to fill the room. Soova reassures grandmother.
She can feel her sweat, she sings fragments of melodies that feel nostalgic to Soova from her childhood.
When the life leaves the body, they dress the body one last time in preparation for viewing and burial, and then Soova sneaks Wazawi out, and then sleeps for the remainder of the day and night.
During the viewing, Migatuka has the blanket around her shoulders, and some kelera tea.
Afterward, the group spends the next couple days gathering necessary materials. Daatsu finds a yutsu bus to take westward, and Tika is the support personnel.
Soova is upset but also knows she has very little time to get a lot together.
Tika is a decent pilot, but hasn’t done rural travel much.
Daatsu is thinking “if I had known, I would have brought x, and y, and z….”
They found Syn in her room, comatose, shaking. Soova recognizes that this isn’t medical, they bring her along and Soova and Migatuka mind her.
