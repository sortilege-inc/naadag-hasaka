## Seeing, Day 2

Still low key. Not really meals, but there’s a trickle of food all day.
Pick-up games of sports, gambling, etc.
Daatsu is oblivious that the woman, Ninosh, he recommended mushroom steaks to keeps coming around and flirting with him, mentions her cooking, very interested in the provenance of the food. Migatuka overhears, and eventually just calls out (slightly buzzed on medicine and a microdose drink) “SHE WANTS TO FUCK YOU.”
The woman turns to Migatuka and laughs. “Was I that obvious?”
Migatuka: “Oh, honey.”
Ninosh blushes, and turns back to Daatsu. “Seek me out if you’re so inclined,” and makes her exit.
Migatuka runs into her colleague, Kaptaanzi. They never really got along.
Migatuka: “Hey, bitch, how’s medical?”
Kaptaanzi: “Better, recently.”
Migatuka: “I’m glad I’m not there.”
Kaptaanzi, judgy: “I can see why. I guess we shouldn’t be expecting you back anytime soon. Don’t worry about it, we’re good.”
Wazawi is glad to have completed her adulthood ritual, but feels out of place both with the kids and adults, and ends up going out into the woods of the big park. She takes advantage of her newfound privileges and camps out under the stars, not asking or telling anyone else what she’s doing.
Tomorrow they’ll begin speeches, and it’ll be more structured; today, with the chaos and lack of structure, Wazawi found it stressful.
Syn ends up being pulled into a game of naasi, blows on the dice to give the uncles luck.
Soova is a little irritated that Wazawi disappeared. She makes her final preparations, as the next couple days won’t allow her. Kooyoopi’s materials are already carefully set out. She then goes to bed.

## Seeing, Day 3

Wazawi arrived, tried to help out. Very interested.
Late morning start. Tables gathered together, into roughly a U-shape. Grandmother is in the center, flanked by immediate family. At various points people stand up and give a speech, or an anecdote, or tell a funny story. Tawihichik, from the Council of Twelve, comes for a few hours and speaks of Naadag’s positive influence in the community.
Soova stands and tells of how meaningful it has been to learn from her; how to raise children. How to honor feelings without having them dictate one’s actions.
There’s a break for a rest/nap mid-afternoon, and there will be a feast in the evening. Daatsu gets pulled in to help prep.
Before the feast is a gift-giving. Syn and Daatsu are giving small handmade trinkets.
Migatuka is given a folded up blanket. She unfolds it and bursts into tears, hugging it close. It used to be Zibizin’s, and her falcon is still prominent, but it’s been amended to appear like a fox. Syn sees this and comes over to comfort. She goes and puts it in the cubby where her things are.
Soova gets a wooden case, which she opens and it is a beautiful jewelry set; an heirloom, and one that would have gone Naadag’s daughter, had she had one. Soova would never have presumed, but this cements the feeling of acceptance and integration into the family that she’s felt.
Soova: “Would it be okay if I wore these during your ceremonies?”
Grandmother: “I would be honored if you would.”
Soova: “I can’t tell you how much this means to me.”
They enjoy the food, the smoked eggs. Nothing starts until tomorrow afternoon, so that everyone can sleep late.

## Seeing, Day 4

Once things get rolling, there’s something of a tag-teamed oral biography of Grandmother Naadag, with three or four storytellers passing the ball between them. The descriptions of her childhood are sparse and vague, and slightly cryptic. In adolescence she arrived with her now-husband in Cahokia. He died about 15 years ago. His death was a more muted affair.
It’s obvious to Syn that there’s an omission, but gets a [failed roll] strong feeling that it is something that feels too unsafe even to mention; that she experienced something in her relationship to the black, the void, that she is seeking to escape, or hide.
Then there’s a series of speeches.
Soova gives a speech about how Grandmother Naadag’s lost child helped her with the loss of her child, how not to get lost in grief.
Wazawi: this is the first seeing that she’s been present for this portion of. Listening intently. The early parts of the narrative feel weird, relatable but also hard to picture, especially since it was nearly 100 years ago.
They go out to watch the sun set, and then Grandmother Naadag retreats.
The evening ends in more quiet conversations, as small groups peel off, build fires, etc.
Wazawi asks Soova if she can participate or watch the next few days. Soova chides her, explains that she can be of assistances afterwards only.
Each gets sought out at a certain point, and told that Grandmother Naadag wishes to speak with them.
They gather around, a little confused. Fur curtains part as grandmother enters.
“Thank you. I have a request I ask of you. It is not something that can be done alone, which is why I ask this of all of you.”
She grasps Syn’s hands. “We have only just met, but you have the eyes that can see what needs to be seen, but the understanding will come from others. I trust your sight.”
She moves to Soova, and clasps her hands: “Like you, I do not come from Cahokia originally. Few know that, during the All-Tribes War, my husband found me on his way back. I have not been to Dine since I was a child, but I have something that I need returned to my ancestral lands.” She turns and hands Soova a sealed box. “I ask that after I am gone you would bring this back to its home.”
Turning to Daatsu, she says, “Your family speaks highly of you, and have consented to loaning you to me. Your skills are highly praised. I have a song for you.” And she proceeds to sing what is an antiquated song-map. Typically used for traversing large swathes of plain. At the refrain, when it begins to repeat, Daatsu join in. As they finish, she says, “Thank you for guiding my daughter back home.”
She gets to Migatuka. “Soova will need a healer. She can heal others, but she must not neglect herself. You are to make sure she is able to return in safety and health. Would you do that for me?” Migatuka agrees.
Everyone but Soova is dismissed.
Syn does not want anything to do with the box. Feels it is dangerous, or sacred.
Daatsu feels like it’s just cargo, but the song map; it goes farther west than he’s ever been, deep into the Dine Republic. It’s a complication he’s never dealt with before. Song maps are antiquated, haven’t been used in a generation or two; it’s unusual that he knows them.
Grandmother: “What I told Migatuka is only half true. She is struggling, and needs a healer of the soul. Time to recuperate, and space away from temptations, may be of help to her. I hope you two can be of aid to one another.”
Wazawi sneaks in, determinedly, and hides in a closet overnight, sleeping standing up, so as to be present in the morning. [massive stealth success]
