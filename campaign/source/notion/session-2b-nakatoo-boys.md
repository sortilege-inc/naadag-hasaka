## Dagamot

Trait - Hermit: At the edge of the fire, lagging behind. Others go to them for advice, but they don’t proffer it. Will periodically disappear without explanation, and show back up a few days later.
Goal - Ten of Swords, reversed: Powerful feelings, finality of defeat. Hitting rock bottom, and liberatory clarity of being so soundly defeated. Once was a person of means, and significance (Suryata?) and is using this for a new start.
Barrier - Four of Pentacles, reversed: Greediness, stinginess, defensive. Or giving away too much of yourself. Trying to let go of those material concerns, and haven’t been able to; everyone in the caravan is hustling, new projects just to get by. That ethos is completely counter to what they were looking for.
References: Shoresy
There is a reticence to offer their perspective, because their perspective is at odds with the hustle, and because they feel that their past failures indict them.

## Hasu

Trait - Emperor: Hasu is the de facto leader of the Nakatoo caravan. Ideas never quite fail, but they never succeed fabulously. Like the SV guy on his fifth start-up (this time!). Never quite clears the hump. Talks a big game, setting that vision. People are willing to entertain/humor his ideas, and while they’re never successful by his standards, they’re successful enough to get them to the next thing. Edge of legality.
Goal - Seven of Pentacles: The six is the ‘end’ of hardship. 7 is return on investment. He’s looking for the one big idea that will allow him to sit back and coast, enjoy success.
Barrier - The Abyss: Some are drawn to the darkness. The N-S land is, by treaty, no-man’s land. There is something there which draws Hasu there, but there is greater risk, unclear reward, but it becomes a competing priority. (How bigly do I bet?).

## Sanguinet

Trait - Ace of Wands: Promising future, something from nothing. Ludicrously big ideas that are completely disconnected from reality. It will be interesting. (cf. Bubble’s cat day-care). The ideas are big, and he’s completely into it, but they also just do not work. The community loves/humors him.
Goal - Page of Pentacles, reversed: Foolish, procrastinator. Not trying to actively misuse the resources, but more investing on things based of off how he feels in the moment. They want to follow through, but seem poorly disposed for doing it.
Barrier - Temperance: Balance, peace, patience. Success is incidental. They aren’t driven enough, too low key. People like this about them, but it’s why they never quite follow through or finish what they start. They are content, satiated.
