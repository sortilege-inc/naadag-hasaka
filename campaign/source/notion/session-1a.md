## Seeing, Day 1

Soova: I’d be really focused on how my family is doing, check in on the grandkids. But the burden of being there for grandmother and showing up well for that responsibility would be in the back of my mind, but initially playing hostell.
When Soova sees Migatuka, she comes over, says hi. They keep to small talk. Soova tries to be welcoming, but not spook her. Seeing Soova brings a feeling of loss back to Migatuka. When Soova offers an embrace, Migatuka apologizes “I don’t want to take away from the Seeing, but I would like to apologize as well. I shouldn’t have waited this long.”
Syn shows up, and definitely stands out - however, there are people from all different aspects of Grandmother Naadag’s life, so it is expected that not everyone will know everyone.
Syn dives back into the spirit world to try to understand why she was brought here. She goes to a park across from the event, and meditates. [Quiet Mind] Syn is expecting an affirmation, but instead gets a vision.
It’s of a pueblo. It’s populated, but definitely not modern. She walks around, feels there for a while. No one interacts with you. Eventually, she turns and it clicks; this is Oraibi - but in the past. Maybe this is prehistory?
Syn goes back in, begins introducing herself to Soova. Soova offers to bring Syn in to meet Grandmother Naadag, and Syn realizes this is who she was seeking - and that Soova is Dine.
Grandmother is doing ok for her age, but is clearly failing. Mostly lucid, seated on a sedan. Can get up/move around with support.
Grandmother clasps Syn’s hands as they speak. “I see you are a beaver. I have a gift for you, but it will need to wait.”
Syn: “I understand, it was a pleasure meeting you.”
Daatsu is trying to make himself useful, but doesn’t really have much to do except to eat the food or wander around. There’s about 50 people so far.
Bumps into someone else, makes small talk. Tries to hype up the buffalo, she explains she’s vegetarian. He pivots to talking about mushroom steaks.
