# overview

## The saga
@ id: premise
Grandmother Naadag, an elder of the Diné Republic who had lived most of her life in Cahokia, chose the
day of her own death and held a Seeing for it. The characters came to it by different roads. At its end
she asked them, in a private meeting that was not recorded, to carry a box to Oraibi, in the Diné
Republic. They leave Cahokia on the morning of the eleventh day with Syn asleep and shaking, and with no
way to wake them. [SET]

The trip is a week, a week and a half with stops. Nothing about the delivery is urgent, and they are not
trying to rush it. [SET — Notion] They navigate by a song map. [SET — session 1/C's recap] Daatsu
navigates; Tika is hired as an escort and works with him. [SET — Notion]

*Naadag Hasaka* is Chahi for *Crow Mesa*: *Naadag*, crow; *Hasaka*, mesa. [SOURCE — the book's glossary]

## The Seeing
@ id: seeing
The Story Guide's invention for this saga [YOURS]. A Seeing is for a respected elder choosing their own
death, and runs ten days:

- Days 1–2: arrivals, informal greetings, food, games. Nobody has to be there at the start.
- Days 3–4: the long tables — speeches, stories, the gift-giving and the feast (day 3); the life told by
several storytellers, then speeches by leaders and elders (day 4).
- Days 5–6: the elder withdraws with one or two attendants to prepare; the guests are quieter, reflect,
rest. The kitchens close.
- Day 7: the death at dawn, privately. The attendants take a blocker against the compound burned in the
room; it stops the elder's body.
- Days 8–9: the body is viewed, then buried or cremated; mourning.
- Day 10: farewells and dispersal. Anyone not gone by the end of it is expected to be.

About two hundred people came to Grandmother's, counting children. Not everyone is given a Seeing; her
husband Misyooyi's death, about fifteen years before, was quiet. (Soova's timeline puts it in 689; the
1/B session notes say about fifteen years, and the chronicle follows the notes.) The death is by smoke:
a mixture of plants burned into a fume that slows and stops the body, trivially negated by the right
medicine taken first. [SET — 1/C notes]

## The world at this table
@ id: world
### The Black
Everyone experiences the Black in their own way, and the same person may see it differently at different
times. [SET] Syn: a translucent overlay on the world that darkens the deeper they go, until they lose
where they are and stop hearing speech. Soova: a spirit body that moves while her senses are set aside,
hazy until she focuses on a place. Migatuka: vivid, dreamlike, first person, and she reads it as her own
subconscious rather than anything outside her. The Black is a rhizome: everything touches everything in
another dimension, which is how some spooky action at a distance works. [SET — Notion]

### The wild corridor
A broad north–south stripe of unoccupied territory, kept for animal migration, just before the Diné
Republic; off limits by treaty. No one lives in it; you can cross it — "Burning Man ethos, leave the
site"; a DMZ without the extreme penalties? Who is breaching the treaty? What it is called is a Notion
GM question. Not in the book. [YOURS] [OPEN] (2/A notes; a map of it is on the 2/A page.)

### The way west
From Cahokia to the destination by way of Coyote City: in our world's terms, Memphis to Albuquerque —
down the Mississippi and up the Arkansas by water, then across the plains past the Wichita Mountains and
the Caprock Escarpment, the Sangre de Cristo to the north, to the Rio Grande valley under the Sandias.
On the plains: migratory caravans, solar-powered pop-up villages, buffalo ranchers, treeless grassland
with trees only along the streams; circuit justice, gangs, the Suyata. [YOURS — 2/A notes]

### Getting around, keeping in touch
Few wheeled vehicles; most things float or hover (yutsu barges), and there are paths more than roads.
The daso is regional: Cahokia has its own, which interconnects with others in the Free Lands; the Diné
Republic's is separate. There may be no wireless version — something more like sneakernet or carrier
pigeon. A niisi on the wrist texts and reaches the local daso; a Diné niisi needs reworking to talk to
Cahokia's. You always stop for someone on foot. [SET — sessions; Notion]

### Drink, and kelera root
There is no alcohol culture. The everyday drinks are brewed from herbs and fungus, microdoses that even
children take, like weak beer once was; stronger brews and distillations go up to "don't operate heavy
machinery". [SET] Kelera root is the base of many of them: it raises blood flow, dulls pain, and is
mildly hallucinogenic. Kelera-adanadi, the root the Adanadi has marked, is the nearest thing to a
controlled substance: it cannot be grown reliably (growers make the conditions and hope; there is
research into it), and for someone who already carries the Adanadi it has a resonance that heightens
what they feel. Migatuka will not have regular access to it. [SET] Substances are not banned as such;
someone who is a danger to themselves or others through use can be made to accept support. [SET]

### Food
Vegetarianism exists; veganism does not really, since what it avoids mostly never came over or was never
made. [SET]

### The year and the war
710 is the present. The All Tribes War ran 612–623. If Grandmother was 121, she was born in 589 and was
23 when the war began. The Diné Republic (Dinada) has a National Council, kivas and clans. [SET — Notion]

# rules

## Stats for a Skill
@ id: rule-stats
A Skill with Ranks uses the higher of its two Stats; a Skill at 0 uses the lower. [SET — as played, 1/A
and 1/C; the book]

## Finding your way in a new city
@ id: rule-survival
Orienting in an unfamiliar city is Survival with Intelligence instead of Endurance. [SET — 1/A]

## Net below zero
@ id: rule-critical
A Check with net fewer than zero Successes is a critical failure: the character does not get what they
wanted and makes things worse for themselves. [SET — 1/B; the book]

## Player death
@ id: rule-death
A PC doesn't die unless the player allows it. [SET — Notion, Palette]

## Goals and Legendary Ranks
@ id: rule-goals
Each player should have two short-term goals and a long-term goal; a goal must use the Skill in question
and may be mentored to expedite. Legendary Ranks; add Group Burdens. A Rank 6 item should take six
sessions, bypassing a Wealth check. [SET — Notion]

## Stars and wishes
@ id: rule-stars
At the end of a session anyone may name a star (a moment they loved) and a wish (something they'd like
to see). [SET]

## Grandmother Naadag
@ id: p-naadag
Nearly 121 at her death; born 589 in the Diné Republic. Orphaned at the end of the All Tribes War; the
man who became her husband, Misyooyi, found her on his way back, and she came to Cahokia with him in her
adolescence. "I have not been to Diné since I was a child." Misyooyi died about fifteen years before her;
she lost children of her own. A portrait of her is on the site. Ceremonial more than spiritual. Honoured by the
Council of Twelve for neighbourhood work copied across the city. Related to the Ancients in Oraibi. [SET
— Notion; the recordings]

She knew Soova's past for many years and protected her, largely without Soova's knowledge, from the
people who came to make her finish her work; she told Soova so before she died (in a letter, or in the
last days — Notion leaves it open). [SET — Notion] Connected to the Coyote crime lords, as Soova and Syn
are. [SET — Notion]

Her plan, from the 1/A GM notes: she understands she can no longer protect Soova from the crime family
Soova escaped, so she sends the party into Dinada — for several reasons, one being to give Soova a clean
slate, a pardon. Daatsu gets them there with a song map to an ancient city older than the Diné (their
devices will not work inside Dinada). Syn and Tika are to be the left and right eyes. Migatuka is told
to tend Soova's health; really it is a detox trip. In play (1/B) she gave Soova a sealed box to return to
her ancestral lands, sang Daatsu the song map ("Thank you for guiding my daughter back home"), told Syn
"I trust your sight", and asked Migatuka to bring Soova home safe and well — then told Soova alone that
this was "only half true". [SET — Notion]

Notion GM questions: Was she Soova's great-aunt, and does Soova learn it? Why is sending Soova intended to
be a pardon? What did she ask them to bring to Oraibi, and why? What did she give Syn? [OPEN]

The early part of her life, as the family tells it, has deliberate gaps. Syn came away convinced they
concern Grandmother and the Black: something she met there and has been getting away from, dangerous
enough that it is better not to know there is anything to avoid. That reading came from a critical
failure and may be wrong. Who reached Syn through the Black with the family's name is not established.
