# overview

## The saga
@ id: premise
Grandmother Naadag, an elder of the Diné Republic who had lived most of her life in Cahokia, chose the
day of her own death and held a Seeing for it. The characters came to it by different roads. At its end
she asked them, in a private meeting that was not recorded, to carry a box back to the Diné Republic.
They leave Cahokia on the morning of the eleventh day with Sign asleep and shaking, and with no way to
wake her. [SET]

The trip is about a week and a half with stops. It is not a busy route, nothing about the delivery is
urgent, and they are not trying to rush it. [SET — the prep note] They navigate by a song map. [SET —
session 3 recap]

*Naadag Hasaka* is Chahi for *Crow Mesa*: *Naadag*, crow; *Hasaka*, mesa. [SOURCE — the book's glossary]

## The Seeing
@ id: seeing
The Story Guide's invention for this saga [YOURS]. A Seeing is for a respected elder choosing their own
death, and runs ten days:

- Days 1–2: arrivals, informal greetings, food, games. Nobody has to be there at the start.
- Days 3–4: the long tables — speeches, stories, the gift-giving and the feast (day 3); the life told by
  several storytellers, then speeches by leaders and elders (day 4).
- Days 5–6: the elder withdraws with one or two attendants to prepare; the guests are quieter, reflect,
  rest. The kitchens close.
- Day 7: the death at dawn, privately. The attendants take a blocker against the compound burned in the
  room; it stops the elder's body.
- Days 8–9: the body is viewed, then buried or cremated; mourning.
- Day 10: farewells and dispersal. Anyone not gone by the end of it is expected to be.

About two hundred people came to Grandmother's, counting children. Not everyone is given a Seeing;
her husband's death, about fifteen years earlier, was quiet.

## The world at this table
@ id: world
### The Black
Everyone experiences the Black in their own way, and the same person may see it differently at
different times. [SET] Sign: a translucent overlay on the world that darkens the deeper she goes, until
she loses where she is and stops hearing speech. Suva: a spirit body that moves while her senses are set
aside, hazy until she focuses on a place. Makatooka: vivid, dreamlike, first person, and she reads it as
her own subconscious rather than anything outside her.

### The wild corridor
A broad north–south stretch of the continent, west of Cahokia, is kept wild by general agreement as a
migration path; people go into it only when there is a need. Datsu's people herd near it. Not in the
book. [YOURS]

### Getting around, keeping in touch
Few wheeled vehicles; most things float or hover, and there are paths more than roads. Networks are
regional and each state runs its own; out in the Free Lands, communities have their own or chain from
place to place, and there are long stretches with no signal. Most people wear a small device on the
arm that texts and reaches the local network. A Diné device needs reworking to talk to Cahokia's. You
always stop for someone on foot. [SET]

### Drink, and kalera root
There is no alcohol culture. The everyday drinks are brewed from herbs and fungus, microdoses that even
children take, like weak beer once was; stronger brews and distillations go up to "don't operate heavy
machinery". [SET] Kalera root is the base of many of them: it raises blood flow, dulls pain, and is
mildly hallucinogenic. Kalera root that the Adanadi has marked is the nearest thing to a controlled
substance: it cannot be grown reliably (growers make the conditions and hope; there is research into
it), and for someone who already carries the Adanadi it has a resonance that heightens what they feel.
Makatooka will not have regular access to it. [SET] Substances are not banned as such; someone who is a
danger to themselves or others through use can be made to accept support. [SET] Spelling of *kalera* not
confirmed. [OPEN]

### Food
Vegetarianism exists; veganism does not really, since what it avoids mostly never came over or was
never made. [SET]

### The year
Zibizin went missing in 703, "seven years ago", so the Seeing is in 710. [SET — session 1] Wasawi was
fourteen in session 1 and sixteen from session 2 on; the table settled on sixteen. [SET]

# rules

## Stats for a Skill
@ id: rule-stats
A Skill with Ranks uses the higher of its two Stats; a Skill at 0 uses the lower. [SET — as played,
sessions 1 and 3]

## Finding your way in a new city
@ id: rule-survival
Orienting in an unfamiliar city is Survival with Intelligence instead of Endurance. [SET — session 1]

## Net below zero
@ id: rule-critical
A Check with net fewer than zero Successes is a critical failure: the character does not get what they
wanted and makes things worse for themselves. [SET — session 2]

## Stars and wishes
@ id: rule-stars
At the end of a session anyone may name a star (a moment they loved) and a wish (something they'd like
to see). [SET]

# people

## Grandmother Naadag
@ id: p-naadag
Nearly 121 at her death. From the Diné Republic; arrived in Cahokia in late adolescence; husband died
about fifteen years before the Seeing; lost children of her own. Ceremonial more than spiritual.
Honoured by the Council of Twelve for neighbourhood work copied across the city. [SET]

The early part of her life, as the family tells it, has deliberate gaps. Sign came away convinced they
concern Grandmother and the Black: something she met there and has been getting away from, dangerous
enough that it is better not to know there is anything to avoid. That reading came from a critical
failure and may be wrong. [OPEN]

She was protecting Suva without Suva's knowledge, and that protection has faltered. [SET — the prep
note] Who reached Sign through the Black with the family's name is not established. [OPEN]

## Ninosh
@ id: p-ninosh
An accomplished cook, vegetarian, interested in where her food comes from. Flirted with Datsu all of
day 2; he did not notice until Makatooka said it aloud. "Seek me out if you're ever so inclined." [SET]

## Choyan
@ id: p-choyan
Early fifties, knows current cybernetics research well. Disproved several of Tika's hypotheses in one
conversation. Tika has made a note of the name. [SET]

## Yohipa
@ id: p-yohipa
Tika's older sister, about 25. Runs a successful restaurant in Cahokia that Grandmother helped her start;
her kitchen was one of several feeding the Seeing. Owes Tika money she can't pay before he leaves. The
Story Guide suggested an indirect link: Makatooka may know the restaurant without knowing Tika. [OPEN]

## Zibizin

ways in session 1 ("both my daughter and her partner are missing"; "my daughter died two years ago");
Grandmother and the chronicle treat the daughter as dead. [OPEN]

## Makatooka
@ id: pc-makatooka
@ about: Makatooka
### At the table
28, Healer, Motivation Altruism. A physician on leave of absence because of her addiction; strongest in
Knowledge, with science and medicine, some herbalism; can pilot (Zibizin taught her). Gift: Spirit World
Connection. Burden: Addiction ("uppers and downers", "the good stuff"; now kalera). Companion: a
hairless cat, Wings. Packed a trauma kit, her herbal knowledge in her wrist device, a raven keepsake.
Left Grandmother's blanket in safe keeping. Performance 0, Spirit 4. [SET]

### Private: the lost child
Only the Story Guide and her player know this; the public pages do not tell it. [NOTE]

She miscarried about seven years ago, close to when Zibizin vanished, and her addiction took hold then.
Grandmother's blanket — a falcon reworked into a fox — is understood by Makatooka in connection with
that. On day 5, on a doubled kalera tea, she saw the tea overflow a bowl and, under it, the child she
imagines she would have had, rising for air; she threw the tea and the bowl was empty. She tried to hide
it and did not manage. [SET]

Prep note: the miscarried child "is going to be an oracle to her", and she "will have to use deception
to avoid the others figuring out what that's all about". "How soon until Makatooka hits withdrawal." In
session 3 the Story Guide checked with the table about discussing the miscarriage in play; confirm
before bringing it forward. [YOURS — the prep note] [OPEN]
